Pixel editor

Draw with the left button and rub out with the right one, or pick the empty swatch. Drag to keep drawing. The export below is the format the build script reads, so it can be pasted back here or into the script.

The lasso works the way the Finder's rubber band does: press on the ground and drag, and everything the rectangle covers is caught. Then drag inside the catch to carry it, or nudge it a cell at a time with the arrow keys. Escape lets it go.
