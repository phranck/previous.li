# Sound effects

These five files come from the sound effect set that [HyperFrames](https://github.com/heygen-com/hyperframes) ships in `skills/media-use/audio/assets/sfx/`. HyperFrames sourced them from [Pixabay](https://pixabay.com/sound-effects/), and they are used here under the [Pixabay Content License](https://pixabay.com/service/license-summary/), which permits commercial use and modification without attribution.

- `click.mp3` is the press on the Merge button.
- `key-press.mp3` is the Return after the install line.
- `pop.mp3` is each of the three facts arriving.
- `typing.mp3` is the install line being typed.
- `whoosh-short.mp3` is each logo arriving.

`../../soundtrack.py` mixes them into `../effects.flac` at the times the cue sheet in `../../index.html` gives.
